Hi, I'm Alfero.

I'm a platform engineering leader, architect, and builder based in the Toronto area. I spend most of my time thinking about developer experience, software delivery, cloud platforms, and what agentic AI can do when it's grounded in real engineering constraints instead of hype.

I've been building software and leading delivery work for more than two decades. Over the years that has taken me through architecture, DevOps, cloud modernization, engineering leadership, and product delivery. The thread running through all of it is pretty simple: I care about making it easier for teams to ship good software without drowning in friction, fragility, or tool sprawl.

That is the lens behind this site.

Professionally, my work sits at the intersection of **Platform Engineering, DevEx, cloud automation, and AI-enabled software delivery**. In practice that means things like CI/CD, observability, feature flags, architecture patterns, self-service platforms, and the organizational work required to make those systems actually useful.

Outside of work, I'm building and sharing projects like **[CueMarshal](https://www.cuemarshal.com)** and **FireFly**.

- **CueMarshal** is my take on a self-hosted, Git-centric AI DevOps platform.
- **FireFly** is a visual code stepper and AI tutor designed to help kids actually understand how code executes.

More recently, I've also been spending a lot of time with self-hosted agent systems, local AI infrastructure, and the practical side of running tools like OpenClaw in the real world.

So if you spend time here, you'll mostly find writing about:

- platform engineering and developer experience
- agentic AI in practice
- DevOps, cloud architecture, and delivery modernization
- building in public
- the occasional career or leadership lesson when the work behind the work feels worth talking about

I also care a lot about mentoring, teaching, and helping other builders grow. Over the years that's shown up through leadership roles, mentoring, guest lecturing, and community work.

This blog reflects my own work, experiments, and opinions.

If any of that overlaps with what you're building, feel free to connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/achingono/), follow along on [GitHub](https://github.com/achingono), or say hi on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/achingono).
